## 2. Root Cause Analysis: Why Do the 51 Orphaned Directories Exist?

During previous sprint development (Phase 3 through Phase 5), automated tests (such as `tests/test_cli_automation_phase5.py`, `tests/test_collector.py`, and `tests/test_manual_ingest.py`) executed ingestion and batch runs against temporary in-memory or throwaway SQLite databases.

Because early test fixtures did not isolate the `BENCHMARK_EVIDENCE_DIR` environment variable to a `tempfile.TemporaryDirectory()`, the collector's secondary file-mirroring mechanism wrote real raw evidence `.md` files and `.meta.json` sidecar files into the project's root `evidence/raw/<batch-id>/` folder.

When the temporary SQLite databases were destroyed at test completion, the on-disk evidence mirrored to `evidence/raw/` remained behind.

## 4. Evidence Integrity & Provenance Verification

- **SHA-256 Content Addressing:** Every captured response in `evidence/raw/` maintains an accompanying `.meta.json` sidecar. With the addition of Migration 002 (`execution_mode`), all future mirrored metadata files will also track `execution_mode: 'live' | 'fixture' | 'dry_run' | 'mock'`.
- **Database Cross-Referencing:** Foreign keys in `review_responses.attempt_id` enforce 1-to-1 matching with `review_attempts.id`.
- **Duplicate Ingestion Handling:** The collector and `ResponseRepository` calculate SHA-256 hashes of the review payload and verify idempotency keys to prevent duplicate submission of the same review text.
